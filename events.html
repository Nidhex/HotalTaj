Your Palace Wedding Awaits

"Exchange vows where emperors ruled, under hand-carved pavilions reflecting centuries of love, heritage, and romantic splendor."

A wedding or gala at RajMahal Palace is not simply an event; it is an epoch. Our majestic spaces provide stunning backdrops for intimate luxury exchanges or massive, high-profile multi-day celebrations. Experience custom ceremonial entries on majestic carriage horses, vibrant floral arches handcrafted from local roses, and exquisite candlelit dining setups under the stars.

Our in-house royal celebrations planning team handles every single detail—from custom invitations written in gold leaf script, to organizing Michelin-standard catering, world-class musical ensembles, and luxury suites logistics for all your esteemed guests.

Majestic Enclaves

Our Celebration Venues

Grand Open-Air Courtyard

The Maharani Courtyard

Surrounded by towering palace sandstone facades and ornate arch balconies, the courtyard offers a jaw-dropping open-air stage. This is the premier location for majestic wedding fire mandaps, grand entries with flower canopies, and lavish banquets.

Capacity 350 - 500 Guests
Aesthetics Historic, Symmetric, Epic
Perfect For Main Nuptials • Sangeet Gala
Expansive Royal Lawns

The Mughal Gardens

Perfectly sculpted formal lawns lined with sparkling stone water fountains, majestic marble paths, and fragrant white jasmine borders. Suited for massive, high-profile celebrity banquets, live orchestral concerts, and luxury weddings.

Capacity 600 - 1,000 Guests
Aesthetics Lush, Botanical, Majestic
Perfect For Grand Receptions • Concerts
Luxury Glass Ballroom

Sheesh Mahal Ballroom

An indoor ballroom masterpiece featuring thousands of micro Belgian mirrors embedded into carved plaster walls, refracting ambient uplights like a diamond palace. Fully climate-controlled for opulent indoor feasts or formal business gatherings.

Capacity 150 - 250 Guests
Aesthetics Glittering, Opulent, Majestic
Perfect For Royal Dinners • Luxury Galas
