Signature Spa Therapies

Abhyanga Royal Oil Therapy

₹12,500 / 90 mins

A classic synchronized full-body massage performed in absolute unison by two trained therapists. Using deep strokes and warm, customized Ayurvedic herbal oils (infused with sesame, cedar, and rosewood), this signature therapy detoxifies bodily tissues, lubricates joints, and enhances skin glow.

Shanti Tibetan Sound Healing

₹9,500 / 75 mins

An acoustic meditation and cellular re-alignment treatment. Over fifteen heavy hand-hammered Tibetan bronze bowls and therapeutic planetary gongs are placed strategically on and around your energy meridians. The resonant, complex vibrational frequencies slow brainwaves to theta states, inducing a profound sense of peace.

Prana Mountain-View Hatha Yoga

₹4,500 / 60 mins

A private hatha yoga and breathwork session led by our resident Guru on the palace’s high marble watchtower pavilion at sunrise or sunset. Accompanied by refreshing cooling herbal drinks, and a panoramic visual sweep of the Aravalli hills range. Suitable for all skill levels.
