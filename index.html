Pranam & Welcome

A Palace Built for Royalty

"Step into an exquisite era of Rajputana luxury, where every hallway whisper-chants tales of ancient emperors, and every custom service is curated with deep-rooted Indian hospitality."

Initially constructed in 1729 as a private sanctuary for the Royal Dynasty of Jaipur, RajMahal Palace has opened its majestic arches to welcome discerning globetrotters. We preserve the original marble frameworks, intricate fresco hand-carvings, and vast courtyards while weaving state-of-the-art five-star amenities into your stay.

From your ceremonial flower petal welcome on arrival, to our signature 24-hour private royal butler services, every detail of your visit is tailored to provide a transcendent experience. Allow us to make your stay unforgettable.

A Symphony of Royal Flavors

Signature Venue

The Durbar Hall

Step under grand gold-leaf chandeliers and hand-painted ceiling frescoes at The Durbar Hall. Our culinary master chefs have revived centuries-old kitchen notebooks of Jaipur's royal household to present dishes prepared with rare saffron, hand-pounded spices, and local gold foil garnish.

"Savor the flavors of authentic Lal Maas, Royal Biryani, and handmade pista kulfi that were once served solely to monarchs."
